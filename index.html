Selected work

Featured case · Duke, spring 2026

The Impunity Index

With Shreya Mendi and Andrew Jin, I built an NLP pipeline over 1.4 million DOJ documents from the Epstein files to measure the gap between how much documentary evidence exists about each person named and whether anything ever happened to them.

Corpus
1,413,024 DOJ/EFTA documents across 5 releases
Scored
1,264 people named in the public record
Method
spaCy NER, sentence embeddings, classifiers trained on 66 hand-labeled people
Built with
Python, scikit-learn, Flask, Google Cloud Run

About

I started on the policy side, studying how technology shapes society and where the guardrails fail, which took me to trust and safety work at Tremau and AI ethics at Metaphysic. As AI started moving faster than regulation, I realized the real decisions get made inside the product cycle, so I learned to build.

Most of what I make follows the same pattern: find a system that makes decisions about people, measure what it actually does, and put the gap somewhere people can see it. I hold an MEng in AI and a BA in Public Policy from Duke.

Evals and LLM-as-judge labeling · NLP pipelines · agent workflows · AI risk assessment · Python, TypeScript, PyTorch, Hugging Face, FastAPI, GCP
