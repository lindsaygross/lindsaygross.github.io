About

Systems thinker with policy and engineering fluency

I am a Master of Engineering candidate in Artificial Intelligence at Duke where I design, train, and evaluate machine learning systems—from structured data pipelines and explainable ML models to RL, deep learning, LLM fine-tuning, and AI agents that support safety workflows.

Before pivoting into AI engineering I earned a BA in Public Policy with a Digital Intelligence Certificate and Minor in Spanish. That lens grounds every product decision in governance realities and user outcomes, so I can stress test features, trust layers, and mitigations before they reach production.

My sweet spot is translating between technical, product, policy, and legal stakeholders. I prototype with engineers, articulate risk to executives, and align with trust and safety partners so responsible AI work becomes shippable.
